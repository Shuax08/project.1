Tables include `shop`, `user`, `customer`, `menu_item`, `order`, `order_item`, `credit_account`, `credit_transaction`, `payment`, `printer_settings`, `audit_log`, `plan`, `subscription`, `bot_session`, `whats_app_channel`, and `incoming_message`. `whats_app_channel.phone_number_id` maps a Meta business phone ID to a shop; `incoming_message.message_id` deduplicates webhook retries. Order items retain historical name, price, quantity, and line total. The initial migration is in `migrations/versions/`.
